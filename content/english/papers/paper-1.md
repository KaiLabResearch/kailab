abstract: "This study adapts an Arabic Tesseract LSTM model to printed Sorani Kurdish using limited Kurdish ground truth and synthetic font-based training material. The authors report 95.45% average character accuracy on an evaluation set of 11 court-document images. A separate Tesseract evaluation reports 2.93% CER and 13.52% WER for the kur_ara model; these results come from different evaluation protocols."
