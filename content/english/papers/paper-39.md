abstract: "This dataset paper introduces K-PIT and K-ZHMARA for isolated Central Kurdish handwriting recognition. K-PIT contains 245,000 character images across 35 classes and K-ZHMARA contains 70,000 digit images across 10 classes, for 315,000 images in total. The publication presents the resources as datasets rather than as a complete word- or line-level OCR system."
