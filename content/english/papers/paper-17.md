abstract: "Develops an extractive automatic text summarization approach for long-form Sorani Kurdish research documents. The study uses a corpus of 231 scientific papers from four academic departments, applies Sentence Weighting and TF-IDF in two experiments with and without conclusion sections, and evaluates the generated summaries using ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, ROUGE-L and expert assessment."
