abstract: "This 2024 arXiv preprint adapts Tesseract 5 for historical printed Sorani Kurdish publications using transcribed lines from pre-1950 material. The abstract reports 1,233 line images with transcriptions, while the currently inspected partial release contains 1,203 TIFF images and 1,203 text files. The paper reports 0.755% CER in its Tesseract workflow and 84.02% average character accuracy in a separate Ocreval assessment."
